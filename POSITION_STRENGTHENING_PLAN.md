## PRIORITY 1: Certification (Immediate Credibility Boost)

### Option A: IAPP AIGP (AI Governance Professional) ⭐ RECOMMENDED

**Why this one:**
- Most recognized AI governance certification globally
- Directly covers EU AI Act (updated 2025)
- IAPP is the "gold standard" for privacy/governance professionals
- Growing demand as EU AI Act enforcement begins August 2025

**Details:**
- **Cost:** ~€450-750 (exam + training)
- **Time:** 4-8 weeks preparation
- **Format:** Online exam, multiple choice
- **Validity:** 2 years (CPE credits to maintain)

**Curriculum covers:**
- AI technology fundamentals
- EU AI Act and global AI regulations
- Risk management strategies
- Ethical AI frameworks
- Governance implementation

**Resources:**
- Official training: [iapp.org/train/aigp-training](https://iapp.org/train/aigp-training)
- Udemy prep course: "AI Governance Professional (AIGP) 2025-26" (~€50-100)
- Exam registration: [iapp.org/certify/aigp](https://iapp.org/certify/aigp)

**Action Steps:**
1. Register for IAPP membership (~€100/year) - gives exam discount
2. Purchase AIGP study materials or training course
3. Study 1-2 hours daily for 4-6 weeks
4. Schedule exam
5. Add "AIGP" after your name on LinkedIn immediately after passing

**Value:** This single certification immediately signals "serious AI governance professional" to recruiters. Many job posts now specifically mention AIGP.
