What Makes This Training Unique

Unlike generic compliance courses, this program is grounded in evidence. Every module draws from authentic compliance failures discovered during a comprehensive codebase audit. Participants don't just learn what the regulation says—they see exactly how systems fail compliance, why those failures occur, and how to fix them. The curriculum includes actual code examples, audit findings, and remediation strategies from the EUAICA project.
